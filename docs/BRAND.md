# CommonKit brand

## Positioning

**Shared gear. Accountable money.**

CommonKit is a community equipment library with refundable bonds and DAO-governed damage disputes. Its first audience is campus clubs and small equipment-sharing communities. The brand should make borrowing feel familiar and the money rules easy to inspect.

Short description: **Reserve community equipment, record the handover and settle damage disputes through two council votes on NEAR.**

Name: **CommonKit**, one word, capital C and K. “The campus commons” is the supporting descriptor, not a second product name. Keep the existing lowercase *ck* monogram; do not introduce token, coin or shield imagery implying financial guarantees or audited security.

Keep SVG text selectable and readable; do not depend on GitHub loading CSS, JavaScript, animation or external fonts.

Leave at least one monogram-width of clear space around the wordmark. Do not stretch marks. Prefer the real product screenshots for capability claims; captions must state capture date, origin and whether actions were signed or read-only.

## Writing and evidence

Lead with the user's action and its consequence: “One vote leaves the bond in escrow.” Avoid unsupported “trustless,” “secure,” “instant,” “fair,” adoption, audit or win claims. “Two council accounts” is accurate; “two independent people” is not established by the demo.

Use **refundable bond**, **usage fee**, **retained storage**, **available credit** and **withdrawal** consistently. Settlement allocates credit; it does not by itself pay a wallet. Use testnet NEAR units, not dollar equivalents.

Screenshots and saved checkpoints show recorded state. Only a fresh verifier result is a current check. Test counts are dated evidence, not a comparative quality score. Do not turn competition research into a public attack on another builder.
