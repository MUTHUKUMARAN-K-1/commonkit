Status: **tester arranged by owner; no independent results collected yet.**
An automated browser check is not a substitute for this trial.

Send only the following section to a person who has not used CommonKit. Do not
explain the product first or guide their clicks. They need no wallet or test funds.

## Message for the tester

Please open https://commonkit-testnet.vercel.app on the device you normally use.
Spend about five minutes exploring. Don't connect a wallet, sign anything, or
send money. We're testing whether the demo explains itself, not testing you.

1. In your own words, what is this product for, and who would use it?
2. Find an example where the equipment comes back normally. Who gets paid, and how much?
3. Find an example where damage is disputed. What happens after one vote? What happens after the second?
4. Find evidence that money was actually paid, rather than just approved.
5. Can you tell which information was recorded earlier and which was checked now? What would an unavailable blockchain connection mean?

Please reply with:
- Device and browser (no personal account details).
- Your answers and roughly how long each task took.
- Any place you got stuck, plus the page URL or wording.
- The most confusing word or control, if any.
- Whether you needed help. “Couldn't find it” is useful feedback.

If you encounter an error, note what it says; do not repeatedly retry a slow RPC
check. No screenshot, login or personal data is required.
