# Five-minute first-visit test

Status: **planned; no independent results collected yet.** This is a test protocol, not a completed study or testimonial. An automated browser check is not a substitute for this trial.

Send only the following section to a person who has not used CommonKit. Do not
explain the product first or guide their clicks. They need no wallet or test funds.

## Message for the tester

Please open https://commonkit-testnet.vercel.app on the device you normally use. Spend five minutes exploring without help. Don't connect a wallet, sign anything, or send money. We're testing whether the demo explains itself, not testing you. “Couldn't find it” is a useful answer.

| Time | Try this | Tell us |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:45 | Explore the first screen. | What is CommonKit for, and who would use it? |
| 0:45–2:00 | Find the example where damage is disputed. | Who decides what happens to the deposit? What changes after the first and second votes? |
| 2:00–3:00 | Find evidence of the resulting payments. | Who received money, how much, and which link supports your answer? Is an approval enough to prove payment? |
| 3:00–4:00 | Find the clean-return example and the booking price breakdown. | What does the borrower get back? Is every part of the booking charge refundable? |
| 4:00–5:00 | Find the verification options. | What was recorded earlier versus checked now? What would an unavailable blockchain connection tell you? |

Stop at five minutes even if tasks are unfinished. You do not need to wait for fresh network checks to finish; record what the page says and how long you waited.

Please reply with:
- Device and browser (no personal account details).
- Your answers and roughly how long each task took.
- Any place you got stuck, plus the page URL or wording.
- The most confusing word or control, if any.
- Whether you needed help. “Couldn't find it” is useful feedback.
- Whether you had used CommonKit before, or seen its walkthrough before this test.
- Whether we may quote your feedback anonymously. Naming you requires separate permission.

If you encounter an error, note what it says; do not repeatedly retry a slow RPC
check. No screenshot, login or personal data is required.
