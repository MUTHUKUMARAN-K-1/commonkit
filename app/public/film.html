The story, in text

Shared gear needs shared responsibility. A campus club lends its camera. If it comes back damaged, who decides what happens to the deposit?

Meet CommonKit: equipment borrowing and community dispute settlement on NEAR testnet.

A borrower reserves the camera with a refundable bond. The custodian records pickup and inspects the return. For a clean return, the full bond becomes borrower credit. Separate withdrawals pay the borrower and the community treasury.

For a disputed return, funds stay locked while the Sputnik DAO considers the damage claim. Evidence hashes help match files; they do not prove what physically happened. One council vote leaves the principal escrowed. The second authorizes the allocation. Withdrawals then transfer the money.
