Borrow. Return. Resolve together.

A real NEAR testnet equipment library governed by a Sputnik DAO. Two council approvals are required to settle disputes and withdraw treasury credits.

Prepare your wallets

Use Meteor Web in Chrome or another full browser. Select Testnet. Import your authorized demo accounts privately in Meteor, then connect them from CommonKit. Private keys never belong in this site or a chat message.

Normal borrowing cycle

  1. Connect the borrower. Choose Campus camera kit and a pickup time a few minutes ahead. Reserve with 0.111 testnet NEAR: 0.001 fee + 0.01 refundable bond + 0.1 retained storage.
  2. Connect the custodian. At the pickup time, select the booking and upload pickup evidence or paste its hash. Click Confirm pickup and approve in Meteor.
  3. Connect the borrower, select the same booking, supply return evidence, and Request return.
  4. Connect the custodian and Accept return. The booking closes, crediting 0.01 NEAR to the borrower and 0.001 NEAR to the DAO.
  5. Connect the borrower and Withdraw available credit. Inspect the confirmed receipt and ledger.

Dispute and council settlement

  1. Repeat reserve, pickup and return request with a new booking.
  2. As custodian, supply damage evidence and Report damage / dispute. The booking shows Council review; its principal stays in escrow.
  3. As either council account, select that booking, supply decision evidence, expand Propose a library change, and propose a settlement withholding 0.003 NEAR. Proposal creation attaches a refundable 0.01 NEAR DAO bond.
  4. In The council’s decision, inspect the target, method, booking ID and amount. Approve once as the first council account: the proposal shows Awaiting votes and funds remain escrowed.
  5. Disconnect, connect the other council account, and approve the same proposal. The booking closes, crediting 0.007 NEAR to the borrower and 0.004 NEAR to the DAO.
  6. The borrower withdraws their credit. A council member proposes a treasury withdrawal; both council members approve. The DAO receives its available fees and damage credits.

Evidence for a rehearsal

These files are clearly labeled synthetic fixtures, not proof of physical events. Save them locally and select the relevant file when asked for evidence: pickup, return, damage, decision. CommonKit hashes files locally; it does not upload them.

If a step stalls

  • Allow the wallet popup. Complete or cancel the request in Meteor, then return to CommonKit.
  • If a transaction has a receipt but is unconfirmed, use Check confirmation. Do not send it again until you inspect wallet activity and on-chain state.
  • Use Refresh after changing wallet accounts. Only admitted members can reserve; only the assigned custodian can accept returns.
  • Pickup opens at the reservation start time. Closed and cancelled reservations do not block a later borrowing cycle.

Testnet only. Gas, retained storage charges and DAO proposal bonds are separate from the fee-and-bond principal ledger. Public visitors can inspect state; only authorized wallets can perform their roles.
