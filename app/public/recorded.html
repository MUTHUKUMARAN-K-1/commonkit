WHY THE COMMUNITY USES IT

The fee goes back
to the shared treasury.

Each completed borrowing cycle pays a usage fee to the community DAO. It can support upkeep and future equipment purchases through the DAO’s own decisions. This demo proves the fee withdrawal, not a real purchase or an operating community.

What the demonstration does—and does not—prove

The 19 transactions are real NEAR testnet actions approved through Meteor on the public Vercel demo. One operator controls both council accounts. The damage evidence is synthetic; its hash does not prove physical condition. There is no independent community pilot or audit claimed.
