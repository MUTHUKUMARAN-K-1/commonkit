Run the testnet demo Wallets, roles & steps
  1. Open this site in Chrome or another full browser. Connect Meteor → Meteor Web and select your testnet account. Allow wallet popups.
  2. As borrower, reserve the camera with a pickup time a few minutes ahead. Deposit: 0.111 testnet NEAR, including 0.1 NEAR retained storage.
  3. Disconnect, switch to custodian in Meteor, reconnect. At pickup time select the booking and confirm pickup with an evidence file or hash.
  4. Switch to borrower and request return. Switch to custodian and either accept the return or report damage with evidence.
  5. For a dispute, a council member proposes the settlement. Each council account connects and approves once. One vote keeps funds locked; two votes execute settlement.
  6. The borrower withdraws their credit. The council proposes a treasury withdrawal and both members approve it.

Use different accounts for borrower and custodian. Both demo council accounts are controlled by the demo operator. Only admitted members can borrow; public visitors can inspect the library. Open the complete demo guide ↗
